LeadOwn

Capture and manage every prospect from first contact through to closed deal. LeadOwn gives you a filterable table of all your leads, structured status tracking, source attribution, and a nurture sequence engine to automate follow-up.

Each row shows the lead's name and email, their current status (color-coded badge), how they came in (source), which agent they're assigned to, and when the record was created. Hover a row to reveal edit and delete action buttons on the right.

The table loads 50 leads at a time. If there are more, a Load more button appears at the bottom.

Adding a Lead

Click New lead in the top-right of the toolbar. A slide-out panel opens with a contact form.

Form fields

Full name required
The lead's full name. Used everywhere in the UI.
Email optional
Must be a valid email address if provided. Used for nurture sequences.
Phone optional
Contact phone number. Free-text, up to 20 characters.
Status optional
Defaults to New. Set to a different stage if the lead already has history.
Source optional
Where this lead came from. Defaults to Manual. See Lead Sources.
Notes optional
Free-text notes from the initial conversation. Up to 2,000 characters.
💡
Only the name is required. You can create a lead with just a name and fill in contact details later via edit.

ℹ️
There is no enforced one-way flow. You can move a lead to any status at any time. Closed leads remain in the table and can be filtered in or out.

Lead Sources

Source tracks where a lead originated. It defaults to Manual and can be filtered in the leads table to see which channels are generating the most prospects.

Manual
Added directly by a team member with no specific origin.
MLS
Came through the Multiple Listing Service or a property portal.
Website
Submitted a form or enquired via your brokerage website.
Referral
Referred by an existing client, colleague, or partner.
Social
Reached out or responded via social media.
Other
Any source that doesn't fit the above categories.

Editing & Deleting

All lead fields can be updated after creation. Deleting a lead is permanent and requires confirmation.

Editing a lead

1
Hover over the lead row.

A pencil icon and a red trash icon appear in the rightmost column.

2
Click the pencil icon.

A slide-out panel opens pre-filled with the lead's current values. Edit any field and click Save changes.

Deleting a lead

1
Hover the row and click the red trash icon.

A confirmation dialog appears showing the lead's name.

2
Click "Delete" to confirm.

The lead is permanently removed from the database. This cannot be undone.

⚠️
Deletion is permanent. Consider moving the lead to Closed Lost instead if you want to keep the record for reporting.

Filtering

Two dropdowns in the toolbar let you narrow the leads table without leaving the page.

Status filter
Show only leads in a specific pipeline stage. Select "All statuses" to remove the filter.
Source filter
Show only leads from a specific origin channel. Useful for measuring which sources convert best.

When either filter is active a Clear filters button appears next to the dropdowns. Click it to reset both filters at once.

ℹ️
Filters apply immediately — no need to press a search button. The table updates as soon as you make a selection.

Nurture Sequences

Nurture sequences let you automatically send a series of follow-up emails to a lead over time.

Each sequence tracks a current step, a status, and a next send date. When a lead is placed in a sequence, the background worker picks it up and sends the appropriate email at the scheduled time.

⚠️
Nurture sequences are not yet available in the UI. The data model and background worker are in place, but the interface for creating and managing sequences is coming in a future release. In the meantime, use the Notes field and manual status updates to track your follow-up cadence.
