FlowDesk

Your AI-powered command centre. FlowDesk combines a live task queue across all your open deals with a Claude-powered assistant that understands your brokerage's current transactions — so you can get answers, draft emails, and stay on top of your workload without switching contexts.

Task Queue

The task queue shows every task across all your open deals in one place, sorted by due date so the most urgent items are always at the top.

Open tab

Lists all incomplete tasks from every deal. Each row shows the task title, the property address it belongs to, and its due date. Tasks that are past due are highlighted in red. The tab label shows the total count — Open (3) — so you can see your workload at a glance without clicking in.

Completed tab

Lists all tasks that have been checked off, sorted by completion date (most recent first). The completion date is shown in place of the due date.

Completing a task

1
Click the checkbox next to any open task.

The task is immediately marked complete with an optimistic update — the UI updates before the server responds so there's no perceived lag.

2
The task moves to the Completed tab.

Both tabs refresh automatically. The open count in the tab label decreases.

ℹ️
Tasks are created on deal detail pages. To add a new task, navigate to the relevant deal via DealPulse → Deals, open the deal, and use the Tasks section. The task will appear in FlowDesk's queue automatically.

AI Chat

The right panel is a live chat with Claude, Anthropic's AI model. It starts with context about your current open deals so you can ask deal-specific questions without re-explaining everything.

Sending a message

1
Type your message in the input at the bottom.

The input expands as you type, up to a maximum height. Shift+Enter adds a line break without sending.

2
Press Enter or click the send button.

The assistant's response streams in word-by-word. A pulsing indicator shows when the AI is thinking.

💡
The AI already knows your open deals. You don't need to paste in addresses or prices — just refer to properties by address and the assistant will use the context it already has. See What the AI Knows for details.

Conversation history

The chat starts fresh each time you load FlowDesk. Your conversation history is not saved between sessions — the AI remembers everything within the current page visit but not across navigations or page reloads.

Example Prompts

The assistant is tuned for real estate workflows. Here are the kinds of tasks it handles well.

Email drafting
"Draft a follow-up email to the buyer's lender on 456 Maple Ave asking for a financing update"
"Write a friendly check-in to the seller at 123 Oak St about the inspection results"
Deal questions
"Which of my deals are at high risk right now?"
"What's the status of the Maple Ave deal?"
Task suggestions
"What should I be doing this week to keep my deals on track?"
"What are the typical next steps after going under contract?"
General knowledge
"Explain what an earnest money release looks like in Texas"
"What contingencies should I push for in a seller's market?"
⚠️
Always review AI-generated emails before sending. The assistant produces drafts — treat them as a strong starting point, not a finished product. Verify all deal-specific details (dates, names, amounts) are correct before use.

What the AI Knows

When you open FlowDesk the assistant is automatically loaded with a snapshot of your brokerage's open deals. Here's exactly what it can and can't see.

DataAvailable to AI
Property addressYes
Deal status (Active, Under Contract, etc.)Yes
Close dateYes
Risk score (Low / Medium / High)Yes
List priceYes
Parties (buyer, seller, agent names)Not yet
Contingency deadlinesNot yet
TasksNot yet
DocumentsNot yet
Closed or cancelled dealsExcluded

The AI snapshot covers up to your 10 most recent open deals. If you have more than 10 active transactions the oldest ones may not be included in the context.

ℹ️
For questions about specific parties, deadlines, or tasks, open the deal detail page in DealPulse and copy the relevant details into the chat. Deeper AI context (parties, deadlines, tasks) is planned for a future release.
