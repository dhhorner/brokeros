DealPulse

Track every transaction from offer to close. DealPulse gives you a live kanban view of all your deals, automatic risk scoring based on deadline proximity, and a structured detail page for each transaction — parties, contingency deadlines, tasks, and documents in one place.

Toolbar actions

Run risk check

Recalculates risk scores for every open deal (Active, Under Contract, Pending Close) right now. The button shows a spinner while running and confirms how many deals were updated. Use this any time you've added or completed a deadline and want an immediate refresh.

2
New deal

Opens the new deal form — see Creating a Deal below.

Creating a Deal

Click New deal in the kanban toolbar. A slide-out panel opens with two sections.

Property fields

Address required
Full street address including city and state. Example: 123 Main St, Austin TX 78701
List price required
The listed asking price in dollars. Must be a positive number.
Beds optional
Number of bedrooms.
Baths optional
Number of bathrooms. Supports half-baths (e.g. 2.5).
Sq ft optional
Total square footage of the property.

Transaction fields

Target close date optional
The anticipated closing date. Used by the risk engine to flag deals approaching close with incomplete contingencies.
Purchase price optional
The agreed-upon purchase price, which may differ from the list price.
Earnest money optional
The earnest money deposit amount.
💡
Only address and list price are required to create a deal. All other fields can be filled in later from the deal's detail page. You cannot change the close date from the detail page yet — set it here if you know it.

After clicking Create deal the panel closes and the new deal appears in the Active column. Click the card to open the detail page and fill in the rest.

Deal Detail Page

Click any deal card to open its detail page at /dashboard/deals/[id].

The header shows the property address, list price, purchase price (if set), earnest money, close date, current risk score, and any active risk flags. A status selector lets you move the deal through its lifecycle.

Below the header the page is divided into four sections: Parties, Deadlines, Tasks, and Documents.

ℹ️
Document uploads are not yet available. The Documents section shows a placeholder. You can track documents manually in Tasks or Deadlines notes in the meantime.

Adding a party

1
Click "Add party" in the Parties section.

An inline form expands below the existing party list.

2
Fill in name (required), role (required), email, and phone.

Email and phone are optional but useful for quick reference from the detail page.

3
Click "Add" to save.

The new party appears in the list immediately. There is no limit on the number of parties per deal.

Contingency Deadlines

Deadlines are the core input for risk scoring. Each deadline represents a contingency that must be satisfied before closing. Track them here and check them off when done.

Adding a deadline

In the Deadlines section, click Add deadline. Select the type, enter a due date, and add optional notes (e.g. contact info for the inspector or lender). Click Add to save.

Completing a deadline

When a contingency has been satisfied, click the checkbox next to it. The deadline is stamped with the completion time and removed from the risk engine's consideration. Completed deadlines appear greyed out.

⚠️
Deadlines directly drive the risk score. An overdue incomplete deadline immediately sets the deal to High risk. Add deadlines as soon as they are agreed upon and check them off promptly so your risk view stays accurate.

Tasks

Tasks are free-form to-dos attached to a deal — scheduling appointments, following up with a lender, ordering surveys, etc.

Adding a task

Click Add task in the Tasks section. Enter a title and an optional due date, then click Add. Tasks can also be assigned (a free-text field for name or initials).

Completing a task

Click the checkbox next to a task. The task moves to a completed state. Tasks with a past due date that are still open are highlighted in red.

ℹ️
Tasks are also visible across all deals from the main Tasks page in the dashboard, where they are sorted by due date so you can see your full workload at a glance.

Risk Scoring

Each open deal gets a risk score — Low, Medium, or High — based on its contingency deadlines and close date.

Low Risk Medium Risk High Risk
Score Triggered when…
High Any incomplete deadline is overdue, OR the close date is within 14 days and at least one contingency is still incomplete.
Medium Any incomplete deadline is due within 3 days (but none are overdue and close is not imminent).
Low No deadlines are overdue or immediately upcoming, and close is not within 14 days with open contingencies.

Updating scores

Scores are updated in two ways:

M
Manually — "Run risk check" button

Click the button in the kanban toolbar to run an immediate check for all your open deals. Use this after adding or completing a deadline when you want the kanban badges to refresh right away.

A
Automatically — background worker

A scheduled worker runs in the background on a regular cadence. When a deal escalates to High, the worker sends an email alert to the brokerage owner automatically — no action needed.

Risk flags

On the deal detail page, specific flag messages appear below the risk badge explaining exactly what triggered the score. For example: "INSPECTION deadline is overdue (was due 5/15/2026)" or "Close date is within 14 days with 2 incomplete contingencies." These flags update every time a risk check runs.

Status Lifecycle

Change a deal's status from the selector on its detail page. Deals move from left to right through four stages.

Active
Offer made, deal being worked
→
Under Contract
Purchase agreement signed
→
Pending Close
All contingencies cleared
→
Closed
Transaction complete
ℹ️
The risk engine only runs on Active, Under Contract, and Pending Close deals. Closed deals are excluded from risk checks and will not appear in the risk check count.

There is no enforced one-way flow — you can move a deal back to any stage if needed. The kanban column updates immediately when the status changes.
